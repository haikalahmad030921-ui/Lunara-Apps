## Brand & Style
This design system captures the tranquil, evocative atmosphere of Indonesia’s diverse archipelagic coastlines. Combining the tactile warmth of sun-bleached shores with the curated sophistication of a high-end travel publication, it bridges nature-centric storytelling and modern utility. 

The aesthetic is an editorial-modern hybrid: it pairs the structural discipline and ergonomics of Material 3 with bespoke editorial flourishes drawn from travel magazines—generous negative space, large photographic bleed horizons, and nuanced typographic hierarchy. The design evokes serenity, wonder, tactile discovery, and reliable guidance for coastal travelers, beachcombers, and conservation-minded adventurers.

## Colors
The color palette reflects natural coastal elements:

- **Primary (`#1B3B48` - Deep Ocean Teal/Navy):** Grounding and authoritative, reminiscent of deep maritime waters and coastal evening skies. Used for prominent headers, primary CTAs, icon accents, and high-emphasis interface chrome.
- **Secondary (`#E07A5F` - Coral Sunset):** A radiant, warm accent derived from tropical horizons and coral reefs. Reserved for key actions, interactive states, badge highlights, and user milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#E8DEC8` - Warm Sand Beige):** A grounding earthy tone representing pristine sands, dunes, and driftwood. Applied to secondary surface backings, filters, and divider accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F5` - Pristine Off-White):** A soft, natural background that avoids digital glare, offering a paper-like reading experience under bright outdoor sunlight.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F4EFE6` - Warm Cream Card):** Sits directly above the base canvas for cards, interactive sheets, and navigational surfaces, providing soft natural separation without harsh borders.

## Typography
The typographic pairing pairs editorial storytelling with clear transactional utility:

- **Headlines & Feature Displays (Playfair Display):** Conveys the romance of vintage travel guides and national park publications. Used for beach names, chapter markers, journal intros, and quote highlights. High-contrast serifs bring organic rhythm to otherwise structured screens.
- **Interface, Metrics & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for high legibility on mobile screens under high ambient sunlight. Its open counters and geometric balance ensure tide charts, weather coordinates, user reviews, and route instructions remain crisp and strain-free.
- **Editorial Subtitles & Overlines:** Always set in uppercase with tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em - 0.08em`) to mimic architectural and museum cataloging styles.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system balances standard mobile ergonomics with the expansive, airy feeling of coastlines:

- **Grid Architecture:** Built primarily on a 4-column layout for mobile (viewports <600px) expanding into an 8-column layout for tablets. Margins remain generous (`1.5rem` standard, condensing to `1rem` on narrow devices) to preserve perimeter breathing room.
- **Hero Bleeds & Editorial Anchors:** Curated photography breaks out of grid margins to span edge-to-edge at key moments (such as beach hero views), transitioning seamlessly into sand-toned surfaces via subtle geometric gradient overlays.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component stacks adhere strictly to multiples of `8px` (`space-sm` for tight data rows, `space-md` for standard card interior flow, and `space-xl` for distinct narrative sections).

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with modern Material 3 and natural lighting principles, depth is primarily communicated through tonal layering and warm ambient shadows rather than stark borders:

- **Tonal Layering:** The base viewport resides on `#FBF9F5`. Floating components, journal cards, and bottom navigation bars sit on `#F4EFE6`. Active floating state sheets utilize pure white `#FFFFFF` with warm undertones.
- **Sunlit Ambient Shadows:** Shadows discard standard harsh grayscale values. Instead, they use a coastal tint (`rgba(27, 59, 72, 0.06)` to `rgba(27, 59, 72, 0.12)`) with extended blur radii and small downward offsets. This simulates soft sunlight reflecting off sand dunes.
- **Glassmorphic Navigation:** Floating bottom bars and top headers leverage a frosted surface blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with an 80% opacity fill of `#F4EFE6`, allowing beach imagery to gracefully diffuse beneath interactive navigation controls.

## Shapes
Shapes take cues from sea glass, pebble stones, and gentle coastal topography. The default roundedness is set to level `2` (Moderate/Rounded):

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** 16px (`1rem`) border radius (`rounded-lg`) creates an inviting, organic silhouette without feeling childlike.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Use smooth capsule or pill geometries (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) for high-touch interactive items like primary CTAs, audio journal triggers, and map filter chips.
- **Media Containers:** Image cards use 16px corner smoothing, frequently paired with deliberate asymmetric layouts (e.g., standard aspect cards juxtaposed with tall 4:5 portrait frames) to reinforce the editorial magazine format.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Sunset Pill):** Background `#E07A5F`, text `#FFFFFF`, full pill radius (`rounded-full`), height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Slight warm elevation shadow on press.
- **Secondary Action (Ocean Outline):** Border 1.5px solid `#1B3B48`, text `#1B3B48`, background transparent. On tap, transitions to `#1B3B48` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Floating Action Button (FAB):** Background `#1B3B48`, text/icon `#FBF9F5`, 56x56px rounded-full, with an ambient teal shadow for starting a new beach journal entry.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Category & Vibe Filters (e.g., 'Snorkeling', 'Surfing', 'Sunset Spot'):** Background `#E8DEC8` at 60% opacity with text `#1B3B48`. When selected, background shifts to `#1B3B48` with text `#FBF9F5`. Pill-shaped, height 32px, text `label-md`.

### Cards (Beach Guide & Journal Entries)
- **Destination Showcase Card:** Background `#F4EFE6`, border-radius 16px, padding `space-md`. Features an edge-to-edge image header with a 4:3 or 16:9 ratio, title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, with tide/weather metrics displayed in neat, small metadata capsules at the top right.
- **Journal Story Card:** Warm sand surface with a subtle left accent line in `#E07A5F`, paired with snippet text in `body-md` and thumbnail photographic previews.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Coastal Discovery Search Bar:** Pill-shaped, background `#F4EFE6`, filled with subtle placeholder text in muted deep ocean teal (`#1B3B48` at 50%). Leading icon for location search and trailing icon for detailed coastal filters (tide height, facility level). No harsh border; uses a 1px border of `#E8DEC8`.

### Lists & Information Modules
- **Tide & Conditions List:** Horizontal tabular presentation with alternating subtle fills, displaying wave height, wind speed, and UV index. Titles set in `label-sm` uppercase tracking with values highlighted in bold Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Elements
- **Interactive Checkpoints:** Rounded check markers (6px radius) utilizing `#1B3B48` fill and `#FFFFFF` iconography when active; unselected state framed by a 1.5px border in `#E8DEC8`.
